02 / HOW IT WORKS

One image.
Two perspectives.

The multimodal pipeline combines what a meme looks like with what its text says, then produces a category prediction.

01

Visual understanding

A Vision Transformer extracts visual features from the uploaded image.

02

Text understanding

OCR extracts Bangla or English text, which is processed using RoBERTa.

03

Multimodal fusion

Visual and textual representations are fused to classify the meme.
